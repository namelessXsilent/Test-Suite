Text vs. Button Background (SC 1.4.3)

Normal text < 24px regular or < 18.67px bold needs 4.5:1. Large text needs 3:1.

Button vs. Page Background (SC 1.4.11)

The boundary of a UI component must have 3:1 contrast against the adjacent color.

Minimum Font Size

No WCAG minimum, but best practice recommends ≥ 16px for UI and body copy. Anything smaller is flagged here.
