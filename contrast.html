Normal Text (SC 1.4.3)

Text < 18pt (24px) regular or < 14pt (18.67px) bold needs 4.5:1 contrast.

Large Text (SC 1.4.3)

Text ≥ 18pt (24px) regular or ≥ 14pt (18.67px) bold needs only 3:1.

Minimum Font Size

No WCAG minimum, but best practice recommends ≥ 16px for body copy.
